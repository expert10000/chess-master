# v1.6.0 — Personal Rating / Strength Model

The Personal Strength Model does not predict or claim an Elo rating. It reports bounded 0–100 evidence estimates for tactical consistency, opening stability, endgame conversion, calculation quality, and opening/middlegame/endgame CPL.

Each estimate explicitly reports low, emerging, or grounded confidence according to relevant reviewed evidence. The monthly trajectory uses only saved, reviewed personal games and shows the overall evidence score available for each month. A missing score is intentionally displayed as missing rather than filled with an assumption.
