# v1.3.1 — Endgame Outcome Tracking

After training a module such as rook endings, start its tracker. The coach freezes the current module-specific endgame CPL and conversion-quality baseline. It then measures only new reviewed personal games added after that point and only positions classified into the same module.

The early outcome appears after five relevant games. **Conversion quality** is the share of reviewed module moves not graded as an inaccuracy, mistake, or blunder; it is not a claim that the game result was solely determined by the endgame. This keeps the loop useful while making its evidence limits explicit.
