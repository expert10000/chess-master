Play Chess Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Play Chess is an independent chess training app. It does not require an account. The bundled Stockfish engine runs on your device.

Information stored on your device

Games and imported PGN files, variations, puzzle progress, downloaded puzzles, and app preferences are stored locally in the app. We do not operate a server that receives these records. The app does not include advertising or analytics services. The app uses no account, contact list, location, camera, or microphone permission.

Optional puzzle download

When you choose “Get new puzzle,” the app sends a request directly to Lichess. Lichess may receive technical request data, including your IP address, and handles it under the Lichess privacy policy. The returned puzzle is saved locally for offline use. The app does not send your game history or puzzle progress with this request.

PGN import and sharing

Import PGN reads the file you select and stores the chosen game locally. Export PGN creates a game file and opens Android’s share sheet. That file goes only to the app or destination you select. The recipient’s privacy practices then apply. Exported copies are outside this app’s control.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Local records stay on your device until you clear Play Chess’s app data or uninstall it. Android backup may copy app data to your Google account if backup is enabled on your device; manage or delete that backup through Android and Google settings. Clearing or uninstalling this app does not remove PGN files you exported elsewhere or requests already received by Lichess.

Security and contact

The app uses Android’s app storage for local records and HTTPS for optional Lichess puzzle requests. Questions about privacy or deletion can be sent to dareks.inf@gmail.com.

Open source

Stockfish is GPLv3 software. This app bundles Stockfish.js 19.0.1; its source and license are available publicly. Included Lichess puzzles are CC0. Play Chess is not affiliated with the Stockfish or Lichess projects.
