# v1.1.0 — Unified Personal Coach 2.0

The coach begins with a single **Today’s home** instead of requiring the learner to scan separate planning, weakness, outcome, analytics and goal panels.

## Daily decision surface

The primary card answers **What should I do today?** and starts the already-composed adaptive plan. It retains v1.0.9 safeguards: due repertoire stays authoritative and the planner preserves an exploration reserve when it uses intervention evidence.

The supporting cards provide the minimum context needed to trust that decision:

- today’s positions, estimated duration and due-memory load;
- the highest-priority recurring weakness;
- the historically most effective intervention method, with its observed CPL change and confidence;
- recent game quality from the latest reviewed-game window;
- the latest measured intervention checkpoint, or an honest collecting-evidence state;
- active-goal progress; and
- current-week training quality.

Each card opens its detailed evidence panel. The dashboard is therefore a compact control surface, not a replacement for analytics or an unsupported causal claim.
