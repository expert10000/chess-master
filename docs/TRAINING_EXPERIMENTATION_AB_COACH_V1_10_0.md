# v1.10.0 — Training Experimentation / A-B Coach

The A/B Coach turns completed recommendation interventions into small, transparent personal experiments.

- It compares two existing intervention families using their measured next-10/20-game outcomes.
- The initial middlegame experiment compares **tactical calculation drills** (blunder drills) with **positional decision exercises** (phase training).
- The suggested next assignment is always the less-tested arm, helping keep the experiment balanced.
- A method is only favored after each arm has at least two measured outcomes and their average CPL gains differ by at least 5 cp.
- Confidence remains insufficient below three observations per arm, developing at three to four, and grounded at five or more.

This is deliberately observational. Game context, changing opponents, and small samples can affect results; the panel reports evidence rather than claiming causation or statistical significance.
